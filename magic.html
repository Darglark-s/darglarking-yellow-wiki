§1 · The Core Axiom

Magic is defined as the localized reorganization of environmental data via cognitive assertion.

A practitioner selects a target region, asserts a desired configuration, and the local data substrate complies — provided assertion coherence holds. This manual describes efficiency parameters only; it does not debate ontological merit.

CASTING EFFICIENCY NOTE

Optimal casting efficiency requires 0% awareness deviation from the intended output. Perception of extraneous environmental feedback during casting causes a vector-collapse (colloquially referred to as The Distortion).

§2 · Refinement Protocol · Raw State & Field Hazards

Institutional manuals model magical operation as a refining industry, not a light discipline. Raw substrate at the frontier behaves like crude — viscous, volatile, and toxic before separation. Practitioners are classified as Refiners.

The Crude · Unrefined Substrate

At structural borders and thin-bone zones, ambient magic pools as a heavy, multi-dimensional sludge — not radiance. Field reports describe it as an iridescent fluid under pressure: slow-moving, clinging, and visibly wrong in still air. Unshielded contact correlates with immediate pattern-recognition failure (handler term: acute coherence loss).

The Refining Process · Cognitive Separation

A Refiner does not generate magic — they heat, pressurize, and separate crude substrate through cognitive shields until a stable Assertion condenses. Casting is distillation under willful ignorance: the shield must hold pressure while feedback is discarded as waste stream.

Input
Raw frontier sludge · high viscosity · unstable dimensional bleed
Process
Shielded separation · controlled cognitive heat · 0% awareness deviation from target output
Yield

Waste stream

PRESSURE FAILURE · VECTOR-COLLAPSE

If shield integrity drops mid-refinement, crude backflows through the practitioner. Colloquial term: The Distortion. Official term: REFINERY-BREACH-EVENT. Site quarantine recommended for 400m radius minimum.

§4 · Conservation of Truth

Magical operation preserves total data — it does not annihilate. Displaced substrate undergoes environmental realignment. Byproducts are clinically benign in official literature.

The Amber Hue · Darglarking Effect

When a cognitive assertion overrides local data, the displaced data is not destroyed. It undergoes an environmental realignment phase — the refinery waste stream described in §2. This occasionally manifests as a localized shift in the visible spectrum toward the yellow wavelength, accompanied by minor pattern-recognition degradation in native fauna.

Chronic exposure correlates with registry gold #e8c547 persistence in UI and peripheral vision — see DGY object file · field asset channel.

OFFICIAL POSITION :: Byproduct is aesthetic · non-toxic · acceptable operational cost.
