A true simulation under this tier is entirely unrestricted — any law that can be imagined may be objectively true within the run. The operator's task is not to react to infinite chaos, but to play the macro game: stabilize, progress, reconstruct.

Boundless Universal Parameters

Unlike fixed principle physics, a Simulation Theory run admits imagined law sets. Gravity may invert; consciousness may compile; luck may be legislated. The hazard is not variety — it is noise without scaffolding. Without bones, boundless law dissolves into unreadable static.

Four Principles · Operative Sensory Kit

Within any Simulation Theory run, temporary imagined laws may mask the underlying code — but all data still behaves according to FIELD · VOID · CHAOS · NULL. These four principles are the operative's sensory kit for technological and scientific progression.

Law Identification
By classifying observed phenomena through the four states, handlers "see through" cosmetic law layers and identify a simulation's true underlying rules — the edge needed when any imagined law could be active.
Progression Mapping
Each principle signals how to advance: FIELD for transmission infrastructure, VOID for designed absence, CHAOS for entropy harness, NULL for horizon reset. Misread the state, misread the era.

Finalized Bones · XK-Class Reality Shift

When idealized bones lock at true end-game, the event parallels an SCP-style XK-Class reality shift — but this is not apocalyptic termination. The weight of finalized bones shifts the entire simulation from an EX entity (unstable raw power, chaotic purgatory) to an EX-S entity (fixed crystallization of that power).

The Shift
Stabilization, not destruction — structured reality replaces volatile EX substrate
The Sanctuary
Living EX entities are not neutralized. Finalized bones provide a permanent, stable environment where consciousness can anchor — chaotic purgatory becomes habitable crystallization. See Mirror Seed · Object 000 · EX-S recovery.

XK NOTE :: In this frame, XK means ascension — the simulation earns a stable address, not a grave.

Archaeology of Conflict · The Unwon Condition

Conflict archaeology is not merely battle history. The winner may decide a world's fate — but a simulation is never truly won while original EX principles and unmapped anomalies remain active in the inherited substrate. Lingering EX elements are the friction that blocks idealized bone reconstruction.
